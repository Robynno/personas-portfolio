Personas & Storyboarding: The Brown Univerity Printer

This project explores the user experience of Brown University students interacting with our campus printers. By conducting observations and interviews, I investigated the experience a Brown student goes through to print!

What Did I Ask?

Expectations

  • When you approach one of Brown’s printers, what do you expect to see on the interface and what process do you anticipate?
  • What options or information do you anticipate readily available on the printer's initial screen?

Experience

  • Describe your most recent experience with using the university's printer.
  • What were the easiest aspects of using the printer?
  • What were the most difficult aspects of using the printer?
  • How intuitive is the printer's layout?
  • Did the interface provide sufficient information about your print job status?
  • Was the responsiveness of the touch screen or buttons as expected?

Motives

  • What method do you use to release your print job (card swipe or email release)?
  • How often do you use the printer?
  • Why do you use the printer?

Challenges

  • What are some of the biggest frustrations you've experienced with Brown printers?
  • Have you ever been unable to complete your printing task due to interface issues? If so, what happened?
  • Do you have suggestions for improving the university printer's interface?
  • What aspects make it difficult to quickly complete your printing?
  • What does the printer do well?

What Did I Find?

General Behavior

People tend to “Swipe → Tap → Print → Go.” They arrive with no device, a phone, or a laptop, and release via email or card.

Expectations & Experience

Users expect simplicity, clear instructions, and an easy-to-tap button for printing.

Motives

Printing readings, lab materials, homework, flyers, shipping labels, etc.

Challenges

Occasional card‐read failures and lack of in‐interface editing options frustrate users.
